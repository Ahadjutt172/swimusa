About EasySwim

Swimming lessons should feel personal.

EasySwim is a private swim coaching service built on one simple idea: the best coaching adapts to the swimmer — not the other way around.

Our Approach

A better swimming experience starts with understanding the swimmer.

Every swimmer arrives with a different story. Some are nervous about the water. Some want to fix a stroke they've used for years. Some simply want their child to feel safe and confident at the pool.

EasySwim builds each lesson around the person in front of us — their ability, their goals, their comfort level, and their pace. No crowded classes. No one-size-fits-all routines. Just clear, focused coaching that moves at the right speed for you.

  • Personalized instruction, every session
  • Clear communication and realistic goals
  • Proper technique from the very beginning
  • A calm, comfortable learning environment

Programs

Coaching built around you.

Four focused coaching paths — each one private, personalized and shaped around the swimmer in front of us.

Home Programs
Program 01

Learn to Swim

For beginners building comfort and confidence in the water.

This program is built for swimmers starting from the very beginning — or returning after a long time away. We start with comfort and breathing, then build toward floating, balance and independent movement.

  • Getting comfortable in the water
  • Breathing and breath control
  • Floating, balance and body position
  • First unassisted strokes and movement
Book a Private Lesson
Program 02

Kids Private Lessons

Personalized instruction designed to help young swimmers develop skills and confidence.

Children learn best when they feel safe, encouraged and never rushed. These private lessons give young swimmers full attention — building water safety, basic skills and genuine confidence at their own pace.

  • Patient, encouraging one-on-one coaching
  • Water safety and comfort foundations
  • Skill-by-skill, encouraging progress
  • Confidence that grows session by session
Book Kids Lessons
Program 03

Adult Lessons

For adults learning to swim or returning to the water with confidence.

It's never too late to learn. Adult lessons are private, respectful and completely judgment-free — whether you're starting from zero or rebuilding comfort in the water after years away.

  • A calm, private, judgment-free environment
  • Comfort, breathing and relaxation
  • Practical water confidence for real life
  • Flexible scheduling around your routine
Book Adult Lessons
Program 04

Technique & Performance

For intermediate and advanced swimmers refining stroke efficiency.

If you already swim but want to swim better, this program breaks down your stroke piece by piece — improving efficiency, timing and breathing so every lap takes less effort.

  • Detailed stroke breakdown and correction
  • Breathing, timing and rhythm
  • Efficiency, endurance and pacing
  • Goal-based progression and feedback
